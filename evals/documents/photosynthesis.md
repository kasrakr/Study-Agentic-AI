# Photosynthesis (Biology 101 notes)

Photosynthesis converts light energy into chemical energy stored in glucose. It happens in chloroplasts and has
two linked stages.

## Light-dependent reactions
These occur in the thylakoid membranes. Chlorophyll absorbs mostly red and blue light and reflects green light,
which is why leaves look green. Absorbed energy splits water molecules (photolysis), releasing oxygen as a
by-product. The energy is captured as ATP and NADPH.

## Calvin cycle (light-independent reactions)
The Calvin cycle takes place in the stroma, the fluid surrounding the thylakoids. It uses the ATP and NADPH from
the light reactions to build sugar from carbon dioxide. The cycle does not need light directly, but it stops
soon after dark because ATP and NADPH run out.

## Overall equation
6CO2 + 6H2O + light energy -> C6H12O6 + 6O2

Carbon dioxide and water are the inputs; glucose and oxygen are the outputs.
