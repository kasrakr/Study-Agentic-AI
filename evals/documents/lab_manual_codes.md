# Lab equipment manual (excerpt)

## CF-900 centrifuge
Maximum rotor speed: 15,000 rpm. Never run the CF-900 with an unbalanced load. Tubes must be placed in opposing
pairs of equal mass.

### Error codes
- ERR-4471: Rotor imbalance detected. Stop the run, open the lid after the rotor halts, and rebalance the tubes.
- ERR-2290: Temperature sensor fault. The chamber may be unable to hold 4 C. Contact service before further use.
- ERR-1018: Lid not fully closed. Close the lid until it clicks and restart.

## Relative centrifugal force
RCF = 1.118 x 10^-5 x r x N^2, where r is the rotor radius in centimetres and N is the speed in rpm.
